Action gate (Industrial & Production Engineers Governor)

HARD holds cannot be overridden. Tolerance containment and stack-up sums are checked by arithmetic on every proposal, at any confidence.

OpGate
:approve-measurementHARD hold unless LSL ≤ measured ≤ USL · interval containment is arithmetic, not judgement

:approve-deviationALWAYS human approval · accepting an out-of-spec part or over-stack assembly anyway
